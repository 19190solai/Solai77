About

Nine years, one spool

Helix Drift is a two person archive working over Hong Kong from helicopters, cranes, ridgelines and whatever rooftop a building manager will open. We started in 2017 with a single roll of Portra and a borrowed harness, and we have not changed the method much since.

The collection is kept in the order it was shot rather than sorted into folders, which is why this page winds rather than scrolls. Plates are printed to order at 40 by 25 cm on cotton rag, and the negatives stay with us.
